About ScentCap

People own great fragrances — and wear two of them.

ScentCap turns a shelf of bottles into a wardrobe you actually use: a daily pick, a fast catalog, and a diary that learns what works.

The problem

Decision fatigue, every morning.

  • Collections grow; rotation shrinks to one or two “safe” bottles.
  • Weather and setting matter — a beast-mode oud at a 38° office is a mistake.
  • Existing tools are databases and forums, not daily companions.

The product

A calm, private wardrobe app.

  • Today — one pick scored on occasion, weather, dress, mood and rotation, with spray guidance.
  • Add — 1,325 real fragrances bundled in the app; add in a tap, offline.
  • Diary & Insights — wears, compliments, cost per wear, rotation health.
  • Layering & Travel — pair bottles, pack smarter.

Principles

Apple-grade, on purpose.

Content first

The bottle is the hero. Interface stays quiet, one accent colour, one primary action per screen.

Native patterns

Large titles, inset lists, segmented controls, sheets — 17pt type and 44pt targets, light and dark.

Local-first

Everything works offline. Data never leaves the device unless you export it.

Roadmap

What’s next.

  • Now — 3.0: full redesign, bundled real-bottle catalog, PWA on every device.
  • Next — iPhone app on the App Store (Capacitor build ready), Home Screen widget for today’s pick.
  • Later — optional end-to-end-encrypted sync, wishlist price alerts, community-sourced catalog updates.
