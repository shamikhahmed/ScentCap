ScentCap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

ScentCap is an offline-first Progressive Web App for fragrance wardrobe management. Your collection stays on your device unless you explicitly export it.

What we collect

  • Nothing by default. No accounts, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers ship in this app.
  • Optional weather context may call Open-Meteo for temperature and humidity — only when you grant location access. Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, not to ScentCap servers (there are none).

Storage

  • App state is stored in IndexedDB and localStorage in your browser.
  • Catalog data is fetched optionally via Fraganty and cached on device — not personalized tracking.
  • Uninstalling or clearing site data removes your wardrobe and wear history.

Network

  • GitHub Pages serves static files only — no server-side access to your collection.
  • Weather lookups are optional and can be denied via browser permissions.

Photos & exports

  • Bottle photos you add stay in IndexedDB on your device.
  • JSON/CSV exports are generated locally — you choose where to save or share them.

Subscriptions

ScentCap Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Subscription status is validated through Apple's StoreKit; see our Terms of Use for billing details.
