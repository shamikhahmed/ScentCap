ScentCap Privacy

Effective 14 September 2026 · Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

ScentCap is offline-first. Your wardrobe stays on your device. There are no accounts, analytics SDKs, ads, or trackers in the shipped app.

What stays on your device

  • Collection, wear logs, notes, and preferences (IndexedDB / localStorage)
  • Bottle photos you add
  • Cached catalog snippets and weather results you requested

What may leave your device

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
Open-Meteo (weather + geocoding) City name or coordinates Weather-aware picks Only after you enter a city or tap Use my location
Fraganty (optional catalog) Search queries Bottle lookup / catalog art When you search the catalog
GitHub Pages Normal static asset requests Serve the app On load / update

Permissions

  • Location — asked only when you tap “Use my location”. City entry works without GPS.
  • Camera / Photos — optional bottle photos. If denied, you can still add bottles without a photo.

Your controls

Export and erase from Settings. Clearing site data removes your wardrobe.

Payments

ScentCap is free. No payments or subscriptions.
