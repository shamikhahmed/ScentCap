Common topics

  • Lost data: Export JSON from Settings before switching phones. Data is local-only.
  • Offline catalog: Real bottles ship with the app for browsing without a network. Optional Fraganty search needs connectivity.
  • Weather picks: Grant location or enter a city for Open-Meteo weather context.
  • Pricing: ScentCap is completely free — no Pro, Stripe, or IAP.
  • Office Safe: Toggle in Settings for desk-friendly recommendations.
  • Bottle images: Bottle images © their respective brands, via Parfumo.
